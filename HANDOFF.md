# EduGrant – Scholarship Finder

## Project Overview
EduGrant is a scholarship finder website designed to help Filipino students discover educational assistance and scholarship opportunities.

## User Problem
Many students have difficulty finding scholarship information because details are posted on different websites and platforms.

## Target Audience
Senior high school and college students in the Philippines who are looking for financial assistance for their education.

## Aesthetic Theme
The website uses navy blue, light blue, and white to create a clean, modern, and professional appearance.

## User Interface Goals
- Provide simple and clear navigation.
- Make scholarship information easy to find.
- Display eligibility requirements and benefits clearly.
- Help students understand the application process.
- Make the website usable on computers, tablets, and mobile phones.

## Planned Website Features
- Home Page
- Scholarship Search
- Scholarship Details
- Student Dashboard
- Application Guide
- About and Contact Page

## Project Mission
EduGrant aims to help students find educational opportunities and make scholarship information easier to access.

**Tagline:** Your Future, Your Opportunity.
